Polymorphism & Pricing Strategy

The rental calculation engine supports swappable pricing strategies via the Strategy Pattern, with automatic polymorphic dispatch across vehicle classes:

  • Polymorphism SUV Surcharge (+15%): Overrides get_rate() to compute base_rate * 1.15 for 4WD & 7-seater vehicles.
  • Strategy Tiered Weekly Rate: Automatically applies a 10% discount for rentals spanning 7 days or more.
  • Encapsulation Late Fee Protection: Returns outside planned schedule automatically apply a 20% daily surcharge penalty.
